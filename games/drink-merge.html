🍹 Drink Merge — How to Play

Goal

Keep the board from filling up. Match drinks to clear them and survive as long as possible.

Basic Rules

👆
Tap a column to drop the current drink into it.
3️⃣
Match 3 or more identical drinks in a row or column to clear them.
⬇️
Gravity pulls drinks down after a clear. Chain reactions give bonus points.
💀
Game over when any drink reaches the top row.

Pressure Bar

🟥
The red bar fills with every drop. When it's full, a new row is pushed up from the bottom automatically — ready or not.
⚠️
New rows arrive every 5 drops. Clear fast or get buried.

Scoring

✨
Each cleared drink = 10 × current level points.
💥
Clear 6+ drinks at once = MEGA bonus.
🔥
Clear 12+ drinks at once = GODLIKE bonus.

Levels

📈
Level up every 8 drops. Higher levels unlock more drink types — making matches harder to plan.
